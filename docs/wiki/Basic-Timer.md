# ⏱️ Basic Time Tracking

---

oXyFlow is designed around one simple rule: **time tracking should be effortless and impossible to mess up**.

---

## 1. The Single Active Timer Rule

In oXyFlow, **only one task can be ticking at any moment**.

Why?

- It prevents accidental double-billing or overlapping entries.
- If you are working on *Task A* and decide to switch to *Task B*, simply click **Play** on *Task B*.
- oXyFlow automatically stops *Task A*, commits its exact duration to your history, and begins *Task B* immediately with zero gaps.

---

## 2. Managing Your Timer

### Starting Work

- Click the green **Play (▶)** button on any task row.
- The top header highlights your current task name, the project color badge, and live minutes.

### Pausing or Stopping

- Click the red **Stop (■)** button in the top bar.
- Your session is saved immediately.

### Working with Subtasks

Need to break a big task into smaller steps?

- Click the **"+"** icon next to a task to create a child subtask (e.g. `Implement Stripe Gateway` under `Checkout Flow`).
- When you track time on a subtask, oXyFlow automatically rolls up total hours to the parent task and project in all your reports.

---

## 3. What If I Forgot to Start the Timer?

Don't worry! You don't have to lose your tracked hours:

1. Go to the **Logs** or **Reports** tab.
2. Click **"+ Add Manual Log"**.
3. Select the task, set the start time and end time (or simply type `45m`), and add an optional note (e.g. *"Phone call with client"*).
4. Click **Save**. The hours are cleanly inserted into your timesheet.

---

## 4. Seeing Today's Work at a Glance

At the end of your workday:

- Look at the **Status Bar** or open the **Reports** tab.
- You'll see your total working hours today (e.g. `6h 15m`) broken down by client and task.
- You can export this overview as a clean invoice summary with one click!

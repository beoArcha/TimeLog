# 🚀 Getting Started with oXyFlow

Welcome to oXyFlow! This guide walks you through downloading, installing, and recording your very first timer session in under five minutes.

---

## Step 1: Download & Install

1. Go to the **[Latest Release](https://github.com/beoArcha/TimeLog/releases/latest)**.
2. Download the package for your operating system:
   - **Windows**: Download `OxyFlow-Setup-x64.exe` and run the installer.
   - **macOS**: Download `OxyFlow-Universal.dmg`, open it, and drag the **OxyFlow** icon into your **Applications** folder.
   - **Linux**: Download `OxyFlow-x86_64.AppImage`, make it executable (`chmod +x OxyFlow-x86_64.AppImage`), and double-click to run.

> **Windows Tip**: If Windows shows a blue *"Windows protected your PC"* message, don't worry! This is normal for open-source software without commercial corporate certificates. Simply click **"More info"** and then **"Run anyway"**.

---

## Step 2: Create Your First Project

Projects represent your main buckets of work — such as a client name, a freelance contract, or a personal hobby.

1. Open **oXyFlow**.
2. Click the **"+ New Project"** button on the left sidebar.
3. Enter a project name, for example: `Client Website`.
4. Choose a color tag (such as `Emerald` or `Indigo`) to easily spot it later.
5. Click **Save**.

---

## Step 3: Add a Task

Tasks are the specific things you are working on today.

1. Hover over your new project in the sidebar and click **"+ Add Task"**.
2. Type your task title, for example: `Design Homepage Mockup`.
3. Press **Enter** or click **Add**.

---

## Step 4: Start Tracking Time

1. Look for the green **Play (▶)** button next to `Design Homepage Mockup`.
2. Click **Play**.
   - The status bar at the top immediately turns active and starts counting seconds.
   - The elapsed counter increments live.
3. When you are done or taking a break, click the **Stop (■)** or **Pause** button.

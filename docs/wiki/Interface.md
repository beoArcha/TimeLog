oXyFlow gives you complete control over how it looks and fits on your screen. You can customize the color theme, text size, and window layout in seconds.

---

## 1. 12 Curated Color Themes

Open **Settings $\to$ Appearance** to select your favorite theme:

- **Standard Modes**:
  - `System`: Automatically follows your operating system's light/dark mode.
  - `Dark`: Deep, restful dark theme for low-light work and evenings.
  - `Light`: Crisp, clean paper-white theme for daytime clarity.
- **Color Accents**:
  - `Slate`: Elegant steel-blue neutrality.
  - `Indigo`: Classic developer dark theme with rich indigo highlights.
  - `Emerald`: Vibrant forest green, perfect for fresh focus.
  - `Amber`: Warm golden tones that reduce eye strain.
  - `Rose`: Subtle crimson and pink warmth.
- **Accessibility**:
  - `High Contrast`: Maximum contrast (WCAG AAA compliant) with sharp borders for readability.

---

## 2. Text and Icon Sizing

Whether you are using a compact laptop screen or a massive 4K monitor, you can adjust the overall UI scale:

- **Small**: High information density. Shows more tasks and calendar items at once.
- **Medium** *(Default)*: Well-proportioned balance for typical displays.
- **Large**: Larger text and big buttons for comfortable reading from a distance.

---

## 3. Window Layout Modes

In the top-right corner of the window, you can switch between 3 window modes:

1. **Compact Mode**: A minimalist floating widget that stays neatly in the corner of your screen, showing only the active timer and a quick switch button.
2. **Medium Mode**: Balanced layout with your project sidebar on the left and timer in the center.
3. **Full Mode**: Expanded panoramic view showing your project tree, active timer, today's timeline, and analytics side-by-side.

---

## 4. System Tray Integration

On Windows, macOS, and Linux, you can close or minimize oXyFlow to your system tray / notification area:
- Go to **Settings $\to$ Behavior** and enable **"Minimize to Tray"**.
- When you click the window close button (`X`), oXyFlow keeps ticking silently in the tray.
- Click the tray icon at any time to instantly bring the window back!
